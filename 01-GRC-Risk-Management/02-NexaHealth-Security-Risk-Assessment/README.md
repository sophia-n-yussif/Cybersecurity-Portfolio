## Scenario

NexaHealth is a healthcare organization that operates a cloud-based healthcare platform used to manage patient records and healthcare services. The organization supports both on-site and remote employees who access company resources through the cloud environment.

During the past six months, NexaHealth identified several security concerns that prompted a formal security risk assessment, including:

- Three phishing emails that successfully deceived employees.
- A former employee account that remained active for eight days after employment ended.
- Multiple failed login attempts from foreign IP addresses targeting employee accounts.
- Employees with more permissions than required for their roles.
- No multi-factor authentication (MFA) for user accounts.
- Patient information encrypted during transmission but not consistently encrypted while stored.
- Security logs that are rarely reviewed.
- No formal incident response process.
- Backups that have never been tested.
- Limited employee cybersecurity awareness training.

The purpose of this assessment is to evaluate NexaHealth's current security posture, identify risks affecting critical assets, prioritize those risks based on business impact, and recommend appropriate security improvements.
