# Botium Toys Internal Security Audit

## Overview

This project documents an internal security audit performed for Botium Toys, a fictional organization experiencing rapid growth through its online marketplace. The assessment focused on evaluating the organization's current security controls, identifying control gaps, and determining areas where security improvements were needed to support business operations and compliance requirements.

The audit was conducted from the perspective of an internal security analyst reviewing an organization's security posture.

---

## Objective

The objective of this assessment was to evaluate whether Botium Toys had appropriate cybersecurity controls in place to protect its assets, customer information, and business operations.

The assessment focused on:

- Reviewing existing security controls
- Identifying security and compliance gaps
- Evaluating potential risks to critical assets
- Providing recommendations to improve the organization's security posture

---

## Scenario

Botium Toys is a small U.S.-based company that develops and sells toys through both a physical storefront and an expanding online presence. As the company began serving customers internationally, the IT department identified the need to evaluate security practices and maintain compliance with applicable requirements.

The audit reviewed the organization's existing controls against security best practices and compliance requirements.

---

## Assessment Approach

The assessment was completed by:
- Reviewing Botium Toys' audit scope, objectives, and risk assessment documentation.
- Evaluating existing administrative, technical, and physical security controls using the provided controls and compliance checklist.
- Identifying control gaps and areas where security practices did not align with established compliance requirements.
- Assessing the security risks associated with identified control gaps using the information provided in the risk assessment.
- Developing prioritized recommendations to strengthen the organization's security posture and improve compliance readiness.

---

## Frameworks and Compliance Areas Reviewed

The assessment considered:

- National Institute of Standards and Technology Cybersecurity Framework (NIST CSF)
- Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS)
- General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
- System and Organization Controls (SOC) principles

---

## Key Areas Assessed

The audit evaluated controls related to:

- Access control and least privilege
- Authentication and password management
- Data protection and encryption
- Network security monitoring
- Backup and recovery planning
- Physical security controls
- Regulatory compliance
