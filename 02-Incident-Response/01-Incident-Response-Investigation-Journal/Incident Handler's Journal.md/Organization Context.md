# Organization Context

**Organization:** Orion Logistics  
**Industry:** Logistics and Supply Chain

Orion Logistics is a fictional logistics company that coordinates the transportation and delivery of goods for commercial clients. Daily operations rely on employee accounts, email communication, internal business applications, and shared storage systems to support collaboration and decision-making.

The organization manages business-critical information, including employee records, customer shipment data, operational documents, and internal resources. Maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of these assets is essential to ensuring business continuity, protecting customer trust, and supporting secure operations.
