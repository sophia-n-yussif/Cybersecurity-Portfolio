# Organization Context

## Organization: Orion Logistics

## Industry: Logistics and Supply Chain

Orion Logistics is a logistics and supply chain organization that relies on internal systems, employee workstations, network infrastructure, and business applications to support daily operations.

The organization manages sensitive business information, including employee data, operational documents, and internal resources. Secure and reliable network communication is essential to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of these assets.

As part of its cybersecurity operations, Orion Logistics monitors network activity to identify suspicious communication patterns, investigate potential security threats, and support incident response activities.

This network traffic analysis project documents investigations performed on suspicious network activity, including potential malware communication, unusual data transfers, and reconnaissance attempts.
